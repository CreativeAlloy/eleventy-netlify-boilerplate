Ceding sovereign ancestral land to violent extortionists does not confer lawful title upon them; it constitutes an ongoing crime of extortion that must be permanently repudiated.

#### 2. Disqualification Under the Montevideo Convention (1933):
Statehood requires four objective criteria: a permanent population, a defined territory, government, and the capacity to enter into relations with other states. Neither the Palestinian Authority (PA) nor the terrorist regimes of Hamas and Palestinian Islamic Jihad (PIJ) meet these requirements:

#### 3. The Fallacy of the "Tribal Emirates" Alternative:
Proposals suggesting that Judea-Samaria or Azzah can be parcelled out to local clan leaders or "tribal emirates" suffer from acute strategic naivety. Tribal clans in the Levant lack ideological coherence and central military command; they inevitably devolve into feudal warlordism and illicit smuggling cartels that are rapidly co-opted, funded, or conquered by Iranian-backed networks. Stability cannot be outsourced to fragmented tribes.

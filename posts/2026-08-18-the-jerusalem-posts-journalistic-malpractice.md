## Weasel Terminology as a Manipulation Tactic

You will notice that The Jerusalem Post's writers are very careful in how they frame their narrative in order to comply with Israeli editorial laws superficially.

![Instagram slide of TJP's article claiming that "the IDF are under scrutiny" and "critics state this violates international law."](/static/img/tjp_slide5.jpg "Slide 5 of Instagram Variant of TJP's Article")

### Analyzing the Claims
> *"The IDF has come under scrutiny for its actions in Kusra, near Nablus. (...) The military will present a plan to transfer West Bank civilian enforcement to the police. Critics say the plan violates international law."*

The Jerusalem Post does not clarify who has performed said scrutiny, who the critics are, and for what reason they believe international law is being violated. In reality, the IDF is under *constant* scrutiny - because the definition of "scrutiny" is a focused, detailed inspection of something. Antizionists constantly scrutinize the IDF. That does not make any of their spiel morally nor logically sound.

Deliberately omitting the parties exercising said scrutiny is called **Using Weasel Words.** It is done to purposefully smuggle a specific conclusion into the reader's mind, with the intent to easily reject criticism by employing a rhetorical trap called **Plausible Deniability:** "Well, we simply reported what is being said; we never said we *agree* with the claim!"

That is **not** honest reporting. When bringing up external parties "scrutinizing the IDF," these parties must be clearly defined:
1. **Who** are the critics?
2. **Which** specific article, treaty, or statute is alleged to be violated?
3. **What** is the exact legal text, and does the action meet the statutory definition of a violation?

Without these crucial details, the claim is meaningless performative [hedging](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/hedging#:~:text=a%20way%20of%20avoiding%20giving%20a%20direct%20answer%20or%20opinion%3A) coming from a news outlet that refuses to take a concrete stand in the name of vague **pop culture research "values,"** and its abstract, pseudo-intellectual definition of "unbiased reporting."
